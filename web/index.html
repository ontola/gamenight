Skip the setup. Stay in the game.

Without GameNight

  • Waiting through loading screens
  • Finding a game everyone likes that fits your player count
  • Working through menus to set up each session
  • Connecting and assigning controllers again
  • Figuring out who’s Player 2
  • Sitting through video intros

With GameNight

  • Your next game is already loading.Games load in the background. When the next one is ready, switching is instant—without setup menus or video intros.
  • Your controller stays yours.Controllers are linked to players across games, without assigning them again.
  • Games fit your group.Choose from games that match your player count and preferences.
  • Your character comes with you.Player profiles bring your own customized character into supported games.
