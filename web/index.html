Your controller comes with you.

Press Back to return to the lobby. Choose another game, or jump back into the one you paused. Integrated games keep your controller assigned to you, so nobody has to work out who Player 2 is again.

Choose Play next in the catalog. GameNight downloads the game if needed and prepares it in the background. The lobby shows when it's ready.

Put your face in the game.

Scan the lobby QR code with your phone. Draw a face or a hat for your character. You can redraw it whenever you like.

You can change the playlist from your phone too. Pick the next game without taking over the screen.

Open protocol. Open source.

Run GameNight locally without an account. Read the code, change the lobby or connect a game you've made. The protocol is public, and games can keep their own engine.
