Start with a playable game.

Sign in to the developer portal with your email and announce your game. No integration work needed to start. From there you upload builds, follow the review and ask us for help. We can help connect it to GameNight, or you can use the open SDK and guide. Listing is free.

For assisted integration, we agree repository access and code handling before preparing a pull request. Your repository can stay private. You review and merge the changes.

Earn from your game.

Give your game away or sell it. You set the price. Players buy it once and keep it, and one owner unlocks it for everyone on the couch. Selling opens to the first developers soon: announce your game, choose Paid and tell us in the portal that you want to sell.

Keep your engine.

Your game runs as its own process. GameNight sends JSON messages to prepare a session, start it, pause it and clean it up. Your code handles those messages and reports when the game is ready.

SDKs are available for Rust, Godot and C/C++. Other engines can implement the protocol directly.

Make the switch feel good.

Load before showing a window. Keep quiet until play starts. When the player returns to the lobby, pause the action and audio. Resume where they left off.

GameNight supplies the player seats and profiles. Your game owns the score screen and the next round. It should also remain playable on its own.

Test what players will notice.

Run the automated integration checks against your game, then check controllers, audio and window focus on the machines you support. Names, colours and drawn faces are checked separately.

The catalog reports integration results so players can see what works. Add your game →
