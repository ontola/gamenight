One computer. Everyone together.

Host a game night.

You bring the controllers. GameNight gets the games ready.

Windows or MacControllersInternet for setup
  1. 01

    Get GameNight.

    Install it on the computer everyone will play on. Connect it to your TV for the big-screen version.

    Download for WindowsWindows x64 · ≈39 MB↓ Download for macOSApple Silicon & Intel · ≈55 MB↓

    Free, open source, early preview. Games download separately.

  2. 02

    Open your lobby.

    Windows: run the installer, then open GameNight.
    Mac: open the DMG, drag GameNight to Applications, then open it.

    That’s your shared home between games.

  3. 03

    Controllers in. Friends in.

    Plug in or pair controllers with the host computer. Press a button on each to join the lobby.

    On Windows, use Xbox / XInput-compatible controllers.

    Your controller stays linked to your player.

  4. 04

    The games get ready.

    Games install automatically in the background. Hang out in the lobby while the first downloads finish.

    First-time setup depends on your internet speed. Downloaded games are saved for next time.

  5. 05

    Let’s play.

    Walk up to Play in the lobby and press Y. Use Back / Select to return to the lobby when you’re ready for something else.
